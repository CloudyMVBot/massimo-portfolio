Beyond The Light — BTS

Spotlighting: ARTECHOUSE x NASA

Location: NYC & DC

Beyond the Light is an immersive exhibition merging NASA data with cutting-edge visuals and sound. It features a 25-minute film and large-scale installations using James Webb imagery, all projected on ARTECHOUSE's 100-million-pixel canvas.

Led video production and storytelling across NASA and ARTECHOUSE, capturing both the narrative and the engineers/scientists behind the mission.
